Build 1 — Marks calculator

From here on the tasks are bigger. One program, several parts, and decisions that nobody makes for you.

This is not graded. Nothing in this part of the course goes into a mark. What it is for is making sure that when we start loops together, you already have everything else working without thinking about it.

A teacher wants a program that turns three marks into a report line for one student.

WHAT IT MUST DO

  • Ask for the student's name.
  • Ask for three marks out of 10: a test, a project, and classwork.
  • Weight them: the test counts 50%, the project 30%, the classwork 20%.
  • Print the weighted average to one decimal place.
  • Print the grade letter for that average, using the European Baccalaureate bands:
    A  9.0 and above
    B  8.0 to 8.9
    C  7.0 to 7.9
    D  6.0 to 6.9
    E  5.0 to 5.9
    F  below 5.0
  • Print a one-line comment that depends on the grade. You choose the wording.
Sample Output :
    Student name: Ines
    Test mark out of 10: 8
    Project mark out of 10: 9.5
    Classwork mark out of 10: 7

    Ines — weighted average 8.35 — grade B
    Solid work, close to an A.

DECISIONS YOU HAVE TO MAKE

These have no single right answer. Decide, then be able to explain what you decided and why.

  • A mark of exactly 8.0 — which band does it fall into? Make sure your program agrees with your answer.
  • What should happen if somebody types 15 out of 10?
  • Marks can be 9.5, so which type do you read them as?
  • The average will come out as 8.349999999 unless you do something about it. What?

IF YOU FINISH EARLY

Add a fourth mark for homework and change the weights so they still add up to 100%.
Then check: does your grade band code need changing at all? Why not?
