Build 4 — Rock, paper, scissors

One round against the computer. Small on the surface, and the neatest thinking problem of the four.

WHAT IT MUST DO

  • Ask the user for rock, paper or scissors.
  • The computer chooses one at random.
  • Show both choices and say who won.
  • Reject anything that is not one of the three words, with a message that tells the user what is allowed.
    import random
    choices = ["rock", "paper", "scissors"]
    computer = random.choice(choices)

Sample Output :
    Rock, paper or scissors? paper
    The computer chose rock.
    Paper covers rock — you win.

DECISIONS YOU HAVE TO MAKE

  • There are nine possible combinations. Write all nine down before you write any code, and mark each as win, lose or draw.
  • Nine tests would work. Three would also work, if you think about what the three winning combinations have in common. Try to find the shorter version — but a working long version beats a broken short one.
  • The draw case is easy to spot. Handle it first and you have six left.
  • Where does the invalid-input check belong: before the computer chooses, or after? Does it matter?
